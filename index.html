Raian Latif Nabil

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of California, Davis , where I started in September 2023. I am working under the supervision of Dr. Cindy Rubio-González. My research lies at the intersection of software engineering, programming languages, and AI for Software Engineering (AI4SE), with a focus on developing techniques to understand, reproduce, and repair software failures.

Previously, I worked as a software engineer at OpenRefactory, Inc. , a Silicon Valley-based deep-tech software company that focuses on automatically fixing security, reliability, and compliance bugs.

I received my Bachelor of Science degree in Computer Science and Engineering from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology in 2021. For my undergraduate thesis, supervised by Dr. M. Saifur Rahman, I studied sequence comparison methods for phylogenetic tree estimation, exploring both alignment-based and alignment-free approaches.

Apart from these, I like to travel to new places. One of my favorite hobbies is stargazing at places where light pollution is a little lower. I am a sports lover and like to watch and analyze games like cricket, football, etc.

CD-MAWS: An Alignment-free Phylogeny Estimation Method Using Cosine Distance on Minimal Absent Word Sets

Co-Authors: Naser Anjum, Rakibul Islam Rafi, Md. Shamsuzzoha Bayzid, M. Saifur Rahman

A distance measure based on the cosine of the angle between composition vectors constructed using minimal absent words, for computationally inexpensive alignment-free sequence analysis. CD-MAWS resolved phylogenetic relationships similar to or better than state-of-the-art alignment-free methods such as Mash, Skmer, Co-phylog, and kSNP3.

Status: Accepted in IEEE/ACM Transactions on Computational Biology and Bioinformatics, 2023. [PDF]
